## 📁 Featured Projects

### 🚢 [DataCo Global Supply Chain Analysis: Optimizing Delivery Performance and Demand Planning](https://github.com/nandakatmenon/DataCoGlobal_Supply_Chain_Analysis)

**Problem:** DataCo Global, a multinational e-commerce company, faced critical challenges with a 55% late delivery rate and inadequate demand forecasting, impacting customer satisfaction across 5 global regions.

**Solution:** Built comprehensive Tableau dashboards analyzing 180,519 orders to identify delivery bottlenecks and demand patterns.

**Impact:**
- Identified First Class delivery as the biggest failure point (94.48% late delivery rate) despite being most profitable
- Discovered Standard Class as most reliable (37.82% late delivery rate) handling 60% of orders
- Uncovered regional profitability disparities: Europe leads in revenue/order ($175) while North America leads in profit/order ($39)
- Provided 6 strategic recommendations including immediate First Class intervention and Same Day promotion

**Tech Stack:** Python, Tableau, Statistical Analysis
